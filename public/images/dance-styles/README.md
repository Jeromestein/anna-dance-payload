# Chinese dance educational illustrations

Generated with OpenAI ImageGen for the Classes page. These are illustrative
stage-costume and prop examples, not photographs of Academy students or
comprehensive representations of regional clothing. Visible captions identify
both images as AI-generated.

- `folk-costume-illustration.webp`: Han Yangge, Mongolian-inspired,
  Tibetan-inspired and Uyghur-inspired stage costumes, left to right.
- `classical-props-illustration.webp`: water sleeves, folding fan, long silk
  ribbons and stage sword, left to right.

The editorial text is user-supplied. Yangge prop context was cross-checked with
ChinaCulture: https://en.chinaculture.org/2006-11/19/content_584506.htm

Original generated PNGs are retained in the Codex generated_images folder.
WebP assets are resized to 1800 × 600 at quality 85.
