# Anna Liu: Credentials and Advantages

## The Gold Standard: 15 Years of Excellence

Anna Liu is far more than the leader of Anna Dance Academy; she is a trailblazer in the field of dance education. With 15 years of frontline teaching experience, her profound artistic vision and rigorous pedagogical system have established an unparalleled reputation in Tampa and across the United States.

## Elite Artistic Pedigree

- **Authentic Lineage:** A protégé of Professor Gao Jinrong, the founder of Chinese Dunhuang Dance; Anna is a contemporary successor of this prestigious artistic heritage.
- **Global Synergy:** Refined her craft at the world-renowned Joffrey Ballet School in New York and pursued advanced studies at the University of Pennsylvania (UPenn). Her work is a masterful fusion of Eastern grace and Western technical precision.
- **Industry Authority:** Her academy is a designated training center for the China Fengwu Dance Education Research Center, representing the highest global standards in dance instruction.

## Peak Achievements: A Powerhouse of Stage Production

As an influential General Director and Artistic Director, Anna Liu’s work has graced the most prestigious stages in America:

- **Theatrical Pinnacle:** In 2025, she directed the grand production “Jasmine Flower” at the Straz Center for the Performing Arts, one of the premier venues in the U.S.
- **Grand-Scale Vision:** Serving as the General Director for the 2026 Chinese New Year Gala at the iconic Hard Rock Live, delivering a breathtaking spectacle for thousands.
- **International Laurels:**
  - YAGP (Youth America Grand Prix) Top 12 Choreography.
  - Tao Li Cup – Best Choreography Award.
  - Back-to-back State Championships for American High School Cheerleading.

## The Core Advantage: Anna’s Progressive Training System

A proven methodology designed to transform dancers—from 2.5-year-old beginners to adult professionals—into elite performers.

- **A Cradle for Champions:** Her students consistently sweep Championships and Best Technique Awards in major competitions.
