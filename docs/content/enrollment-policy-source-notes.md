## Reconciliation and scope

The website follows the English agreement, including its explicit exclusion of makeup eligibility
for unexcused absences and its written-policy and mandatory-law exceptions to non-refundable fees.

The old website statement that technique training was merely recommended for competition students
was replaced with the supplied competition requirements. The previous approximate term length and
minimum ten-session competition package were omitted from this general policy page because this
agreement does not establish those purchase details; families are directed to the selected offering.
This update does not change booking products, pricing, class catalogs, or other marketing pages.

The website checkbox remains consent to website terms, not a signature on the enrollment agreement,
a participation waiver, or a promotional media release. The website describes the signed agreement's
risk and media provisions without claiming that a login checkbox executes them. The existing Privacy
Policy's optional media choices remain available; this update does not introduce automatic media
consent. No language-precedence clause or legal enforceability conclusion has been added.
