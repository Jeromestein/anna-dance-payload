# Meet Our Director – Anna Liu

**Founder & Artistic Director | Choreographer | Dance Educator | Community Arts Leader**

Anna Liu is the **Founder and Artistic Director of Anna Dance Academy**. She holds a **Bachelor’s degree in Dance Education and Dance Performance** and has more than **15 years of experience** in dance education, choreography, performance, artistic direction, and student development.

Throughout her career, Anna has been committed to combining strong technical training with creativity, artistry, confidence, and meaningful performance opportunities. Her areas of expertise include **Chinese ethnic and folk dance, classical Chinese dance, ballet, jazz, Acro Dance, cheerleading, stage performance, competition preparation, and cultural arts programming**.

## Professional Achievements

In 2026, Anna choreographed and coached *The Dance of the Cloud Fae*, performed by students of Anna Dance Academy, which earned **Top 12 in the Small Ensemble category at the Youth America Grand Prix (YAGP) Chicago competition**.

Anna is also the recipient of the **Best Choreographer Award at the International Taoli World Competition**.

In 2025, Anna served as the **Artistic Director of the dance drama *Jasmine Flower***, presented at the **Straz Center for the Performing Arts** in Tampa.

Anna also has extensive experience in competitive team coaching. She previously coached a high school cheerleading team to a **state championship**, further strengthening her expertise in performance training, teamwork, discipline, and competition preparation.

## Artistic Direction & Performance Production

In addition to teaching and choreography, Anna has extensive experience in **artistic direction, student performance production, choreography, and stage management**.

She has served multiple times as an **Artistic Director, Choreographer, and Performance Organizer** for student productions at the **New Tampa Performing Arts Center**, overseeing choreography, rehearsals, performance preparation, artistic presentation, and stage coordination.

Anna has also been extensively involved in the planning, organization, choreography, and artistic direction of **Chinese New Year, Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, and other Chinese cultural arts productions and celebrations throughout the Tampa Bay area**.
